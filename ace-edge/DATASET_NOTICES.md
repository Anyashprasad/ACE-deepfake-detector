# Dataset notices

ACE Edge is research-only. Obtain datasets from authorized upstream sources and comply with their terms.

- **FaceForensics++:** authorized local copy; private compact derivative, not publicly redistributed.
- **Tiny GenImage / GenImage derivatives:** generator-diverse AI-image research data; preserve attribution and non-commercial restrictions.
- **Unbiased Tiny GenImage:** robustness/encoding-bias stress evaluation unless a separately documented overlap audit changes its role.
- **140K Real and Fake Faces:** auxiliary real/StyleGAN exposure; existing folders are not proof of external independence.
- **SDFVD:** final external video evaluation only; never training, calibration, threshold selection, or model selection.

Do not commit raw media, private local-path manifests, caches, credentials, predictions, or checkpoints.
